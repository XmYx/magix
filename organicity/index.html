ORGANICITY

A pixel-art city builder where nothing snaps to a grid. Draw streets any way you like; every wedge, curve, sliver and corner becomes a lot, and buildings shape themselves to fit.

  1. Roads Branch off the highway (2). Curves, any angle, bridges over water.
  2. Utilities Power, a water pump and a sewage outlet by the shore (4). They flow through the road network.
  3. Zones Paint homes, shops, industry, offices or mixed-use along the streets (3), or fill a whole block.
  4. Growth Buildings grow into their lots. Appealing areas densify and merge lots; neglected ones decline.
  5. Services Fire, police, clinics, schools, parks and landfill (5). Coverage follows the roads.
  6. Tune Taxes (9), overlays (8) and districts with local policies (6).
